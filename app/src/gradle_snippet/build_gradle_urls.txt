Open app/build.gradle and find these two lines inside defaultConfig { }:

    buildConfigField "String", "BASE_URL", '"http://tradingstock.online/api/v1/"'
    buildConfigField "String", "WS_URL",   '"ws://tradingstock.online/ws/prices"'

Change http:// to https:// and ws:// to wss:// — your domain now forces HTTPS
(Certbot auto-redirects port 80 to 443), so plain http/ws requests are being
redirected or silently blocked, which is the main reason data wasn't loading:

    buildConfigField "String", "BASE_URL", '"https://tradingstock.online/api/v1/"'
    buildConfigField "String", "WS_URL",   '"wss://tradingstock.online/ws/prices"'
